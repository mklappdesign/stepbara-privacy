Delete your account

App: Stepbara · Developer: MKL App Design · Effective date: October 8, 2026

Home Privacy Terms Support Delete account

In the app

  1. Open Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type delete to confirm.
  4. Confirm with Apple or Google when prompted.

By email

Email mklappdesign@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We verify identity by replying to that address.

What is deleted

  • Your account, sessions, and sign-in provider links
  • All your step history (daily totals, distances, and goals)
  • Your settings: capybara name, daily goal, reminder, and units
  • Capy Closet purchases and your capybara's outfit
  • Sign in with Apple authorization (when deletion succeeds in the app)

What may remain

  • Database backups up to 7 days
  • Server logs up to 30 days
  • Crash reports in Sentry (not linked to your account)
  • Your steps in Apple Health or your phone's step sensor, which Stepbara never changes

Apple users: After deletion, you can remove Stepbara under Settings → Apple Account → Sign in with Apple.
