Stepbara counts your steps with a capybara who walks with you. Set a daily goal, keep a streak, earn badges, and spend the coins you earn on outfits for your capy in the Capy Closet. On iPhone it reads your steps from Apple Health; on Android it uses the phone's built-in step counter. You sign in with Apple or Google so your history follows you between devices.
