My steps show zero on iPhone

Stepbara reads steps from Apple Health. Open the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Stepbara and turn on Steps and Walking + Running Distance. Steps from your Apple Watch or other apps appear once they reach Apple Health.

My steps aren't counting on Android

Stepbara uses your phone's built-in step sensor, so your phone needs one, and Stepbara needs the Physical activity permission (Settings → Apps → Stepbara → Permissions). Steps are counted while the phone is with you; open the app to see the latest total.

My daily reminder doesn't arrive

Check that reminders are on in Stepbara (Settings → Daily reminder) and that notifications for Stepbara are allowed in your phone's settings. The reminder is only sent on days your goal is still open. Android may deliver it a few minutes late.

I can't sign in

Stepbara needs an internet connection to sign in. Sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used before to see your step history. Google sign-in on Android requires Google Play services.

Delete your account
