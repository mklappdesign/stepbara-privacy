MKL App Design (“we,” “us”) publishes Stepbara. This policy describes what information the app handles.

Summary

  • You sign in with Apple or Google so your step history follows you between devices.
  • Stepbara only reads your steps and walking distance. It never writes to Apple Health.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you.
  • Crash reports go to Sentry and are not linked to your account.

Your account

We store your user id, display name, email (which may be an Apple private relay address), email-verified flag, sign-in provider and its account id, and created/updated times on our server (Microsoft Azure, United States). We never store your password, profile picture, IP address, user agent, or provider tokens.

Sessions use a random token and expiry; expired sessions are purged within 24 hours.

Steps and activity

On iPhone, Stepbara reads your step count and walking distance from Apple Health, with your permission. On Android, it reads the phone's built-in step counter (physical activity permission) and estimates distance from your steps.

The app turns those readings into one total per day. For each day we store the step total, walking distance, the goal in effect that day, your time zone, the source (Apple Health or the Android step counter), and when it was measured. Individual Health samples and the live step count stay on your phone.

Health and step data is used only to show your progress, streaks, history, badges, and coins. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never stored in iCloud. Stepbara does not write to Apple Health.

Settings and the Capy Closet

We store your capybara's name, daily goal, reminder on/off and time, distance units, and when you finished setup, so they sync between your devices. Closet purchases (item, color, coins paid) and your capybara's outfit are stored too. Coins come only from your steps and cannot be bought.

Theme, language, and the reminder schedule stay on your device.

Notifications

The optional daily reminder is scheduled on your device. We do not send push notifications from a server.

Crash reporting

Sentry receives device model, OS version, app version, and stack traces when the app or our server hits an error. Reports are not linked to your account and contain no step data. We disable IP storage in Sentry.

Providers

  • Microsoft Azure — hosting and database (United States)
  • Sentry — crash reports
  • Apple and Google — sign-in only

No ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Retention

Account and step data until you delete your account. Database backups up to 7 days. Server logs up to 30 days.

Deleting your account

See Delete account. Deletion removes your account, sessions, provider links, all step history, settings, Closet purchases, and outfit; Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked when possible. Health data on your phone is not affected.

Your rights

Contact us to access, correct, export, or delete your data. EU/UK: we are the controller; legal bases are contract (your account and the app's features), your consent for reading Apple Health or the step counter (withdraw it any time in the Health app or system settings), and legitimate interests (security and crash diagnostics). You may complain to your regulator. Data is processed in the US. We do not make automated decisions about you.

California: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Do Not Track: we do not respond to DNT signals because we do not track users.

Children

Stepbara is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security & changes

We use HTTPS and industry-standard practices. We may update this policy; the effective date will change.
